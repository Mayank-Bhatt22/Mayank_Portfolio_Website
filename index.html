About Me

I’m Mayank Bhatt, a B.Com III-year student passionate about Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Data Science. I focus on transforming raw data into meaningful insights and data-driven solutions using data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and predictive modeling.

To me, data is more than just a collection of numbers—it is a powerful tool for making better decisions and solving real-world problems. With hands-on experience in Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI, I enjoy transforming complex data into meaningful insights. I’m also continuously expanding my knowledge of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to build smarter, data-driven solutions.

What motivates me most is the real-world impact of data. I don’t just focus on technical analysis—I also believe in communicating insights clearly so they can support better decision-making. Through internships and various data-driven projects, I have gained hands-on experience in data analysis, visualization, and problem-solving.

I have worked to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application through hands-on projects and real-world experience. I’m a quick learner, a team player, and someone who enjoys turning raw data into valuable insights. I’m always looking for opportunities to learn, grow, and contribute to meaningful, data-driven solutions.
